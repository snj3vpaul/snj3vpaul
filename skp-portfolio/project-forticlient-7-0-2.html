01

What it is

FortiClient is Fortinet’s VPN client. Version 7.0.2 became Fortinet’s first VPN certified under FIPS 140-3, the current version of the US and Canadian standard for cryptographic modules.

02

My part

I led the workstream.

  • Boundaries. What the validation covers.
  • Key management. How keys move through the module.
  • Approved mode. How the module behaves when running as validated.
  • Lab liaison. The working line between engineering and the accredited lab.

03

What was produced

  • Algorithm testing through ACVP for AES, RSA, SHA, HMAC and ECDSA.
  • The Security Policy, written to NIST SP 800-140B.

04

Where it stands

Validated: Fortinet’s first FIPS 140-3 VPN certification.
